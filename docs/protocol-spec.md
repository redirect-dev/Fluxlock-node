# Fluxlock Protocol Specification

Version: 1.0 Freeze

Status: Stable

---

## Protocol Purpose

Fluxlock provides continuity-based identity authentication.

Authentication is achieved through continuity verification rather than credential ownership.

---

## Authentication Flow

Step 1

Identity submits request.

↓

Step 2

Lineage verified.

↓

Step 3

Continuity state evaluated.

↓

Step 4

Peer governance review.

↓

Step 5

Consensus vote performed.

↓

Step 6

Authentication decision returned.

---

## Consensus Requirements

Minimum quorum:

3 Validators

Default approval threshold:

Greater than 50%

Weighted governance supported.

---

## Identity Evolution

Identity may evolve.

Evolution creates:

- New Identity Hash
- New Lineage Entry
- New Epoch

Trust remains attached to continuity.

---

## Governance System

Peer validators continuously evaluate:

- Trust
- Reputation
- Continuity
- Legitimacy

Governance influences authority.

---

## Authority System

Authority is earned through:

- Stability
- Consensus participation
- Reputation
- Continuity preservation

Authority is not stake based.

---

## Succession

Authority holders may transfer influence through continuity succession.

Requirements:

- Valid lineage
- Legitimacy
- Governance approval

---

## Protocol Invariants

The following must always remain true:

1. Identity requires continuity.

2. Continuity requires lineage.

3. Governance requires consensus.

4. Authority requires legitimacy.

5. Succession requires continuity.

---

## Protocol Definition

Fluxlock is a continuity-based adaptive identity protocol that preserves trust through change.
