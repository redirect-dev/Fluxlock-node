# Fluxlock Validator Specification

## Validator Definition

Validators are continuity participants responsible for:

- Authentication
- Consensus
- Governance
- Continuity Verification

---

## Validator Responsibilities

### Identity Validation

Verify:

- Signatures
- Continuity lineage
- Credential chains
- Evolution proofs

---

### Consensus Participation

Vote on:

- Authentication requests
- Governance decisions
- Authority elections

---

### Governance Participation

Evaluate:

- Peer legitimacy
- Continuity state
- Reputation

---

## Core Validator Metrics

### Trust

Primary confidence metric.

---

### Continuity Reputation

Historical reliability.

---

### Adaptive Reputation

Evolution success history.

---

### Memory Score

Historical continuity memory.

---

### Consensus Accuracy

Voting accuracy over time.

---

### Governance Weight

Influence in governance decisions.

---

### Authority Points

Accumulated leadership score.

---

## Continuity Requirements

Validators must maintain:

- Valid lineage
- Healthy continuity state
- Consensus participation
- Governance compliance

Failure results in:

- Legitimacy reduction
- Quarantine
- Fracture
- Exile
