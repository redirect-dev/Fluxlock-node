# Phase 20 — What FluxLock Is Not

## Purpose

This phase defines explicit non-claims to:
- Prevent misinterpretation
- Avoid overstatement
- Preserve long-term credibility
- Reduce regulatory and legal ambiguity

FluxLock prioritizes correctness over marketing.

---

## Not a Cryptocurrency Claim

FluxLock is NOT:
- A promise of financial return
- A yield-bearing instrument
- An investment product
- A speculative asset by design

Any token associated with FluxLock exists solely to enforce protocol security.

---

## Not a Quantum-Proof Guarantee

FluxLock does NOT claim:
- Absolute quantum immunity
- Permanent cryptographic safety
- Resistance to all future attacks

Instead, FluxLock minimizes **attack persistence and blast radius** through:
- Time-bound keys
- Behavioral decay
- Economic penalties

---

## Not a Governance Token

FluxLock does NOT rely on:
- Token voting
- On-chain governance politics
- Social consensus mechanisms

Security decisions are deterministic and protocol-driven.

---

## Not a Privacy Coin

FluxLock does NOT attempt to:
- Obfuscate transaction history
- Hide participation by default
- Replace privacy-focused blockchains

Privacy enhancements may exist but are not the primary goal.

---

## Not a Single-Point Defense

FluxLock is NOT:
- A single algorithm
- A single cryptographic primitive
- A static security model

It is a **layered security protocol**.

---

## Not a Replacement for Good Engineering

FluxLock does NOT:
- Replace secure key storage
- Replace hardware security modules
- Eliminate operational risk

It reduces the damage caused by inevitable failures.

---

## Why These Non-Claims Matter

Overclaiming destroys trust.

FluxLock intentionally limits its claims to:
- What can be demonstrated
- What can be simulated
- What can be reasoned formally

This restraint is foundational to adoption.

---

## Summary

FluxLock is designed to:
- Fail safely
- Degrade gracefully
- Recover deliberately

It is not magic.
It is engineering.
